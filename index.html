Dangers to Marine Life

The ocean faces numerous threats from human activities and environmental changes:

  • Plastic pollution and chemical waste
  • Overfishing and destructive practices
  • Climate change and acidification
  • Coral reef destruction

Solutions to Protect Marine Life

  • Reduce plastic use
  • Support sustainable fishing
  • Restore marine habitats
  • Use cleaner energy sources
